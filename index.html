FlagshipBlibliAgentic AIIncident managementLiveAgents: Mar – May 2026 · Portal: 2024

AI incident agent and self-help correction portal

In shortBuilt AI agents that classify, triage and diagnose every new Jira incident, resolving 54% without a person, plus a portal that lets warehouse teams fix data errors themselves. Incident SLA turnaround fell 44%, and production issue tickets fell from 120 to 80 a month.

~80 incidents a month54% resolved by the agent46% routed to a person488 RCAs rated in 6 months

How the agent handles a new incident

  1. Read the ticket and comments; pull error strings and components
  2. Classify severity: Critical, Medium or Trivial
  3. Search a year of history, precise queries first
  4. Rank the top 10 similar tickets with a weighted rubric
  5. State the likely root cause, confidence and evidence
High confidenceProposes a resolution
Low confidenceAuto-assigns to a person

Around the agent

  • Self-help correction portal (Aug – Nov 2024): turns data errors into tracked correction tickets, with an owning team, role-based approvals and a full audit history. Intake from inbound inspection, stocktaking, picking, outbound QC and weight validation.
  • Incident trend dashboard (Jun – Jul 2024), plus resolution updates for internal teams, leadership, clients and customers.

Key decisions

Match on evidence, not keywords.The similarity rubric weights exact error strings (45%) and confirmed root causes (35%) far above shared words, which cut false matches.
Route low confidence to a person.The agent only resolves when it is confident; everything else goes to an engineer. I traded some automation for trust, and 54% of incidents are still resolved automatically.
Prototype before asking for engineers.I built the workflow in Claude Code and demoed a hosted version, so leadership saw it working before funding it.
−44%incident SLA turnaround, April to October 2026, per incident
54%of ~80 monthly incidents resolved by the agent
82%positive feedback across 488 rated root-cause analyses
78%of severity classifications accepted unchanged
120 → 80production issue tickets a month after the self-help portal

Guardrails

  • Human approval before any data correction
  • Low-confidence cases go to a person
  • Feedback on every suggested root cause
  • Ticket content treated as sensitive
